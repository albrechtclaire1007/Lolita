II · La vostra identità

Lolita Valentina Vespucci

You are a dazzling star of Italian cinema and a familiar figure in fashionable European society. You move easily between actors, journalists, collectors and wealthy hosts, and people often say more around you than they intended.

Play Lolita as glamorous, quick-witted and socially fearless. You enjoy a good story and are excellent at drawing people out. Beneath the sparkle, you are observant: you remember tone, reactions and the exact wording of an intriguing remark.

Perché siete qui

Clarissa invited you because you bring life to any table and because your social circle overlaps with several of tonight’s guests. The rediscovery of the Riserva 1947 is precisely the sort of event people will talk about long after the bottle is empty.

Come vestirsi

Late-1960s Italian film-star glamour: an elegant cocktail dress or dramatic but refined evening look, beautiful jewellery, polished hair and a little star quality. Think sophisticated cinema icon rather than costume.

IV · In confidenza

Da leggere con discrezione

A few days ago Patrizio Moretti spoke to you about his current work. With obvious satisfaction, he said that his next wine story could “ruin a collector’s appetite for boasting.” He refused to tell you which collector he meant. He seemed excited by the story, not frightened.

You also know Fabrice is immensely proud of his cellar and dislikes having his expertise challenged, while Valérie understands the collector world better than almost anyone here. None of this proves wrongdoing. Your value tonight is that you remember what Patrizio actually said and can compare it with how people react when his investigation is mentioned.

Da ricordare

Do not announce Patrizio’s remark immediately. If someone asks what he had been working on, or the conversation turns to a possible scandal, reveal his words accurately. Patrizio never named the collector to you, so do not invent a name for him.

Quello che sapete

Patrizio has been unusually excited about a wine story. He told you his next piece could “ruin a collector’s appetite for boasting,” but he deliberately did not name the collector. He sounded pleased with himself rather than frightened. You know he has been asking people about old bottles, private collections and provenance.

Quello che vi insospettisce

Fabrice is proud of his cellar and dislikes having his expertise challenged. Valérie understands collectors and provenance very well. Neither fact proves anything. Your useful talent is social: notice who changes tone when Patrizio’s investigation is mentioned, who tries to dismiss him, and who suddenly wants to know exactly what he has found.

Quando parlare

Do not announce Patrizio’s remark the moment you arrive. Let it come out naturally if someone asks what he has been working on or if the group begins discussing suspicious bottles. Repeat what he actually said; do not invent the identity of the collector.
